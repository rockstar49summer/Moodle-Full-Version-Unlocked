## Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

Moodle is a powerful virtual learning platform designed to facilitate online education for teachers and students alike. It enables educators to create modular teaching and learning websites, making it an ideal solution for educational institutions of all sizes. With its Course Management System (CMS) capabilities, Moodle supports various learning activities, fostering collaborative environments while providing essential tools for assessment and content management. Compared to typical alternatives, Moodle stands out with its extensive customization options and supportive community.
